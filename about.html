About Gobli

A synthetic goblin, one self-hosted sniper bot, no middleman.

What Gobli Is

Gobli is a self-hosted sniper bot for Solana token launches, with two snipers inside. With the Manual Sniper you paste a contract in Telegram and Gobli fires in under 500ms. With the Auto-Sniper you write the rules once and Gobli watches launches itself, buying the ones that match without waiting on you. Either way: no chart-staring, no missing the entry while the candle runs without you.

It comes in two builds. Gobli Free costs nothing: both snipers on a short leash, so anyone can see exactly how it works before paying a thing. Gobli Pro is the full speed version, a one-time purchase.

Beyond the Tools

We also take on seed phrase recovery by hand, for wallets whose owner is only a word or three short of getting back in. Every case is different, so it starts with a conversation, runs under a digital contract agreed up front, and is paid out of what's actually recovered, never before.

Why Self-Hosted

Gobli runs on your own VPS, with a wallet you create and control. There's no custody, no middleman, and no team holding your funds at any point. You sign, you send, you sell.

The Character

Gobli is also 100% AI-generated, the art and the attitude are all synthetic. No real person is depicted or impersonated. Think of Gobli as a living mascot for the project: consistent, a little unhinged, and always in the trenches with you.

What's Next

Gobli Free and Gobli Pro are both live today, and they keep moving. Solana, Jupiter and the launchpads change constantly, so the tools get updated to match, free for anyone who already has them. New venues and new tools land the same way, announced in the Telegram first. See the products section for what's shipping right now.
