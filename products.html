Gobli products

Gobli is a self-hosted Solana sniper bot with two snipers inside: a Manual Sniper that buys a token the moment you paste its contract into Telegram, and an Auto-Sniper that watches pump.fun launches against rules you set in advance and buys the matches with no human in the loop. Gobli Free ($0) runs both on a short leash, in paper mode without a private key if you like. Gobli Pro ($199, one-time) runs both side by side at full speed. Both ship with full source and run on your own machine, so your private keys never leave it.
