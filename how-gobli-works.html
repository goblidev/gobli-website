Try me free. Then go Pro.

I come in two builds. Gobli Free is the full experience at a gentler pace, so you can watch me work before you pay me anything. Gobli Pro is everything, as fast as I go. Both are self-hosted, both run in Telegram, and your keys never leave your machine.

How Gobli Free works

  • Download it from the Products section of gobli.io and unzip it. That's the only place it comes from: a copy from anywhere else may have been changed to steal keys.
  • Open setup.html in your browser. It writes your settings file without touching the internet. Pick Paper only and you don't give it a private key at all.
  • You need two free things: a Helius API key (the free plan is enough) and a Telegram bot from @BotFather.
  • Run it on your PC or a VPS, then send /start to your bot and pick a sniper. 🎯 Manual: paste a contract and I buy it. 🤖 Auto: give me a creator wallet or a ticker and I watch pump.fun launches for it.
  • Paper first. Every trade is priced from live Jupiter quotes and nothing is sent, so you see exactly how I behave. Add a dedicated hot wallet and send /live confirm when you're ready to spend real SOL.

Free is deliberately a little slower and a little smaller: three open positions, one auto rule, and every trade routed through Jupiter and sent the standard way. It's the real thing on a short leash, not a demo.

How Gobli Pro works

Both snipers run at the same time, and you switch each one on or off from the menu. Every position, whichever one opened it, gets the same sell buttons, take-profit, stop-loss and PnL card.

  • Manual: paste a contract and I take the shortest route the coin allows. Still on its pump.fun bonding curve? I build the buy myself and fire it straight at the curve, no aggregator in between. Anywhere else, I route it through Jupiter, with the quote already fetched while I was checking.
  • Auto: write rules once, by creator wallet or ticker, with thresholds that filter out copycat coins. I watch launches as they hit the chain and buy the matches with no human in the loop, inside hard caps per snipe and per day, with a kill switch that sells everything in one command.
  • Every trade goes out through Helius Sender, which races it across staked connections and Jito, over a connection I keep warm so nothing waits to dial in.
  • Dry run first. Pro also starts by simulating every buy against the real chain, so you watch it behave before it can cost anything.

Do I need a paid Helius plan?

No. Helius is the Solana data provider both builds talk to, and its free plan runs both Gobli Free and Gobli Pro: every launch is seen and every buy goes out. What a paid Helius plan changes is how early I see a launch.

  • Helius free plan: I hear about each new pump.fun coin through its public launch log, a moment after it lands. Rules fire, buys go through, you just enter a little later than bots that see launches sooner.
  • Helius paid plan (Developer, about $49 a month, paid to Helius, not to me): Gobli Pro also listens to Helius' pre-execution feed, which shows a launch before it has even finished executing. Upgrade the same key and restart Pro, and it uses the faster feed automatically.

Gobli Free always uses the launch log, whatever your plan. If you're on Helius' free plan, Pro tells you so in Telegram the first time you arm a rule, so you always know which feed is doing the work.
