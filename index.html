Questions

Before You Snipe

Straight answers. If yours is missing, ask @goblidev on Telegram.

What's the difference between Gobli Free and Gobli Pro?

Speed and reach. Both have the Manual Sniper and the Auto-Sniper, both are self-hosted, both start in paper mode. Free runs one sniper at a time, holds three positions and one auto rule, and routes every trade through Jupiter the standard way. Pro runs both side by side with no caps on what you trade by hand, buys pump.fun coins straight from the curve, sends through Helius Sender, and adds unlimited rules, finer filters and PnL cards. Free shows you how I work. Pro is me at full speed.

Manual or Auto, what's the difference?

Who pulls the trigger. With the Manual Sniper you paste a contract into Telegram and I buy it right then, so nothing happens unless you say so. With the Auto-Sniper you write the rules up front, a creator wallet to follow or a ticker to watch, and I buy on my own when a launch matches, whether or not you're at your desk. Both builds have both. Pro runs them at the same time.

Do I need a paid Helius plan?

No. Helius is the Solana data provider I talk to, and its free plan runs both Gobli Free and Gobli Pro: every launch is seen and every buy goes out. A paid Helius plan only changes how early I see a launch. With one, Pro also listens to Helius' pre-execution feed and gets in sooner. Upgrade the same key and restart, nothing else to change.

What can the Auto-Sniper actually buy?

pump.fun launches, live and verified in both directions. It watches new launches as they hit the chain and fires on the ones matching your rules. It starts in dry run, so it walks through the whole pipeline without spending anything until you deliberately turn it live, and it holds caps on how much it may spend per snipe and per day, a cooldown per creator, and a kill switch that drops every rule and sells every position in one command.

Do you ever see my private key?

Never. Both builds are self-hosted and run on your own machine. Your keys stay there. Gobli Free even runs without one: in paper mode it prices every trade live and sends nothing.

Why is Gobli self-hosted?

Because your wallet should stay under your control. We don't custody funds, store keys, or act as a middleman.

Why do you only accept crypto?

Crypto is faster and has no borders. Banks can block, delay, or flag payments depending on your country, and that's not a problem we want between you and your purchase. SOL, ETH and BTC land directly, no middleman, no waiting.

Why is a one-time price cheaper than the "free" bots?

Because free isn't free. GMGN, Trojan, BonkBot and the rest skim ~1% on every transaction, that's ~2% per trade, buy and sell. Cycle just $10k through positions and you've already paid $200. Gobli Free takes no cut at all, and Gobli Pro costs $199. Once. Forever.

Can I move from Free to Pro?

Any time. Message us, pay once, and you get the Pro download. Your Helius key, Telegram bot and wallet carry straight over, and Pro's setup page writes its settings file in a minute.

How does the seed phrase recovery work, and when do I pay?

You tell me what's left of the phrase and what's missing, and I search the combinations until one opens the wallet. One to three missing or misordered words is the realistic range, more only if you have clues that narrow it down. Every case is different, so ask. The percentage and the terms go into a digital contract before I start, the fee comes out of what's recovered, and if nothing opens you owe nothing.

Do the tools get updates?

Yes. Solana, Jupiter and the launchpads all change, sometimes with no warning, so the tools change with them. Buy Pro once and the updates are yours, no subscription and no second invoice. Free gets updated too. New venues and features land the same way, and the Telegram is where they get announced first.

Why are you selling the snipers if they work?

Nobody asks a shovel company why they don't mine gold themselves. Same here. Gobli still requires you to stay active and put your own capital at risk.

Why is Gobli AI-generated?

Because Gobli is designed to be a living digital mascot. AI lets us create new content quickly while keeping the character consistent. The goal here is the tools, not a mascot studio, so we're not burning time or budget on hand-drawn art for something that isn't concretely part of the business.
